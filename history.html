M Shahul Hameed Rowther Sons

1963

Sixty-three years on the same street.

Founded 1963. Registered 1965. One of Coimbatore’s oldest rice wholesale houses, still trading from Rangai Gowder Street, Town Hall.

1963

Where it began

A tiled-roof shop on Rangai Gowder Street. A hand-painted Tamil board above the opening, and rice stacked to the roof behind the counter.

1965

Officially registered

Two years in, the business was formally registered. It is the date most records still carry, and the one the shop has counted from ever since.

1985

Growing with trust

Two decades on, a proper storefront. The name went up in English alongside the Tamil, above a room stacked wall to wall with rice.

1994

Expanding our reach

A glass front, a wider shopfront, more varieties on the shelf — and a reach that carried well beyond Town Hall.

1995

The MS mark

The oval MS emblem went up above the door. Three decades later it is still the mark customers look for on R.G. Street.

Today

Same street. Same trade.

552, R.G. Street, Coimbatore. The board is brighter and the range is wider, but the address has not moved in six decades.

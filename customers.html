Who We Supply

Wholesale Rice Supplier for Hotels, Caterers, Mills and Institutions in Coimbatore

M Shahul Hameed Rowther Sons has supplied wholesale rice from Rangai Gowder Street, Town Hall, Coimbatore since 1965. We sell in bulk to businesses across Coimbatore and the wider Kongu region — from a hostel kitchen taking a few bags a month to a rice mill moving a lorry load.

Over 200 varieties on the floor.

Boiled rice, idli rice, raw rice, basmati, seeraga samba biryani rice and Kerala matta rice. Whatever your trade needs, tell us the variety and the volume and we will quote it.

Every scale of customer

Rice for caterers

Event and wedding caterers buy seeraga samba biryani rice and boiled rice from us in function-sized volumes. Tell us the guest count and the menu and we will work out the quantity with you.

Rice for retailers and provision stores

Kirana and provision shops across Coimbatore buy at wholesale rates and mix varieties in a single load, so a small shop can carry a full range without over-committing on any one type.

Rice for supermarkets

Supermarkets need the same grade in the bag every week. Six decades of buying experience is what lets us hold a variety steady across repeat orders.

Rice for hotels and restaurants

Hotels and restaurants in Coimbatore run on daily volume. We supply boiled rice and raw rice for everyday meals, and biryani rice for the kitchen’s speciality dishes.

Idli rice for batter mills

Batter mills live or die on consistent idli rice, because the grade decides the batter yield and the fermentation. This is one of the varieties we are asked for most often.

Rice for rice mills

We trade with mills across Tamil Nadu and beyond, both as a buyer and as a supplier, which is how the 200+ variety range on our floor stays filled.

Rice for industrial suppliers and contract caterers

Industrial canteen contractors feeding factory workforces need economical rice in steady monthly volume. We quote on the variety and the quantity you run.

Rice for wholesalers and distributors

Other wholesalers and distributors buy bulk lots from us to move into their own networks, using our range and our volume rather than sourcing each variety themselves.

Rice for hostels and canteens

Hostels, college messes and staff canteens have a predictable monthly requirement. Boiled rice in bulk is usually the most economical choice for feeding a fixed number of people every day.

Rice for marriage halls

Marriage halls and function venues order for the event, not the month. Biryani rice and boiled rice in wedding volumes, quoted against your booking calendar.

Rice for institutions

Schools, colleges and hospitals buy on a schedule and need the paperwork to match. We have supplied institutional kitchens in Coimbatore for decades.

Rice for bulk buyers

If you buy rice by the tonne and your trade is not on this list, it does not matter. Tell us the variety and the volume and we will quote it.
